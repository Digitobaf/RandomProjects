# Casey’s Room

[Download Casey’s Room ZIP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Digitobaf/RandomProjects/main/Caseys-Room.zip)

Unzip the download and open **Caseys-Room-with-Mood-Ring.html** in your browser. The complete app, artwork, fonts, and sounds are included in that one HTML file.

Casey’s Room starts on the desktop. Open **Lamplight Write** from its desktop icon or the Start menu when you want to use it; no EPUB is needed to use Diary or Mood Ring.

This version includes the ornate, transparent **A Month in Jewels** calendar and eleven moods, including a **Neutral pearl**. Existing books, Diary entries, and mood history keep their original storage. The fairy swoosh still plays when Mood Ring opens, and **Light Spell** plays when the ring stone changes color. Diary’s code and appearance are unchanged.

**Ruskin** is a separate illustrated bear burrow. Open it from its desktop icon or the Start menu, then click a room to randomly choose one of its two looping activities:

| Room | Activities | Meter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kitchen | Stirring minestrone / eating minestrone | Spoon · Hunger |
| Living room | Drinking tea by the fire / reading by the fire | Heart · Fun |
| Bathroom | Bathing / brushing teeth | Soap · Hygiene |
| Bedroom | Two sleeping poses | Zzz · Energy |
| Study | Writing / drawing at the desk | Star · Creativity |

The painted wooden meters change with his levels; each stat icon is separate from its bar. Snow falls outside, and the fireplace has moving watercolor flames. Your uploaded recordings play as balanced, smoothly joined loops: fireplace for tea, fireplace and page turns for reading, and the matching recording for each other activity. Your bell plays once when Ruskin changes rooms. The mailbox lights up on hover and opens the illustrated bear settings panel: music above sound effects, with two independently adjustable volume sliders. Teddy Bear Waltz plays as looping music and keeps its place across room changes. The mute button covers both sound effects and music. Drag the title bar to move Ruskin; Minimize, Close, and Escape are available. His selected activity, meter levels, position, both volume levels, and mute preference save locally in separate storage. While Ruskin is open in a visible browser tab, a full stat drains in one active hour. The current room replenishes its matching stat from empty to full in two active minutes. Closed, minimized, or hidden time carries no penalty. Lamplight Write, Diary, and Mood Ring are unchanged by Ruskin.

Ruskin’s poses are now drawn with each room’s furniture: seated in the chairs, resting on the pillow beneath the quilt, reaching the desktop, and using the stove, table, bathtub, and sink. Only the small moving areas change between frames; furniture and floors stay still. The desktop icon has the same transparent background as the other programs.

The bell at the end of the uploaded sleeping recording has been cut out. The separate room-change bell remains.

The study uses a compact, properly supported desk in both its empty and occupied views, with relaxed writing/drawing poses, pages facing Ruskin, and fixed furniture during animation.
